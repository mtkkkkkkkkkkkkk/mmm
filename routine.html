3. The rules (aimed at what went wrong before)

Old habitRule now
Studying the night before24-hour rule: every lecture or tutorial gets 20–30 min of review the same day. You learn each topic in the week it's taught, not in February.
Skipping lecturesAttend everything. Circuits says material is taught orally and the course changed this year. Anatomy attendance is graded. A missed class is made up from the recording within 48h, no exceptions.
Skipping weak topicsWeakest first: each day's first deep block goes to the thing you least want to do. Every Friday, list your 3 weakest topics, and they get the first block next week.
Over-relying on AI30-minute solo rule: attempt a problem alone for 30 min (on paper) before any AI. Then ask for a hint, not a solution, and re-solve it alone the next day. Algebra puts an exam question (6%) on the open homework, and Circuits asks you to submit your AI chat log, so solving it yourself is directly worth points.
Passive readingActive recall: close the notes and write down what you remember, then check. Keep an error log: every mistake, why it happened, and the correct idea. Reread it every Friday.
Homework at the deadlineStart on release day, submit 24h early. Late Moodle submissions get 0.

7. Course cards

Calc 2 · 104013 · 5.5 pts

  • Grade: 5% open HW (6 sheets, best 5) + 5% WeBWorK (12, best 9) + 90–100% final. HW counts only if the final is ≥50.
  • Topics: vectors & geometry, limits/continuity, gradient & differentiability, implicit functions, Taylor & extrema, Lagrange, double/triple integrals, line & surface integrals, Green/Gauss/Stokes, conservative fields.
  • Strategy: you've studied most of this before, so use it. Do one past exam question per week from week 4, then full timed past exams from week 10. Keep your own 1-page sheet per topic (method + common traps).
  • Exam: A 24.2 · B 21.3 (clash).

Circuits · 440105 · 4 pts

  • Grade: 70% final + 20% HW + 2% self-study task + 4% + 4% active-learning weeks. All of it counts only if the final is ≥55.
  • Self-study task (capacitors & inductors): opens after lecture 2, closes before lecture 4. Do it this week.
  • Exam: 3h multiple choice with a formula sheet, so speed and accuracy matter. Practice MCQs under time.
  • Topics: KCL/KVL, nodal analysis, Thevenin/Norton, linearization, op-amps, 1st/2nd-order transients, logic circuits, sinusoidal steady state, power, resonance, coupling.
  • This course is your EE foundation and the one closest to the internship you want. Learn it properly.
  • Exam: A 28.2 · B 21.3.

Algebra 2 · 104038 · 2.5 pts

  • Grade: up to 8% open HW + up to 6% computerized HW + 6% exam question based on the open HW + 80–100% final (all מגן, need ≥50 on the final).
  • Open HW due: 18.11 · 2.12 · 16.12 · 30.12 · 13.1 · 28.1. Computerized: 25.11 · 14.12 · 5.1 · 28.1.
  • Topics: inner products, Gram–Schmidt, projections, Riesz & adjoint, normal/self-adjoint/unitary, spectral theorem, SVD, norms, plus bilinear/quadratic forms if time allows.
  • No HW solutions are published, so check your answers in office hours.
  • Exam: A 7.2 · B 7.3.

Physical Chem 1B · 1240503 · 2.5 pts

  • Grade: 85% open-material exam + 15% Moodle exercises (submit 6 of the 8 mandatory ones; counts only if the exam is ≥55).
  • Moodle answers: numbers only, no units, ±5% tolerance. Save drafts and submit early.
  • Binder: each week, one clean page with the week's equations, when to use them, and one worked example. By 28.1 this is your exam weapon.
  • Topics: gas laws, 1st law, enthalpy, Cp/Cv, entropy, Gibbs/Helmholtz, chemical potential, phase diagrams, Clausius–Clapeyron, colligative properties, equilibrium constants.
  • Exam: A 1.2 · B 11.3.

Biochem & Enzymology · 1340019 · 2.5 pts

  • Grade: 15% HW (5 sheets) + 15% midterm (מגן, 18.12) + 70% final. Both count only if the final is passed.
  • Read the assigned "Biochemistry Free For All" pages before each Wednesday lecture (15–20 min).
  • Topics: interactions & water, amino acids, protein structure, purification, Hb/Mb & cooperativity (Hill), enzyme kinetics (Michaelis–Menten, kcat, Km, inhibition).
  • Daily 10-min flashcards (amino acids, structures, kinetics equations).
  • Exam: A 15.2 · B 15.3.

Intro to Human Anatomy · 2 pts

  • Grade: 70% final (need >55) + 15% HW/questions + 15% attendance.
  • Flipped class: watch the recorded lecture and upload 2 questions on Monday night (deadline ≈ 48h before class), attend Thu 10:30–12:30.
  • Use the "entrepreneurship question" each week to practice thinking like a medtech engineer. It's good material for interviews.
  • 21.1: Anatomical imaging (CT, MRI, US, X-ray). Directly on your target field, so take good notes.
  • Exam: A 23.2 · B 18.3.
